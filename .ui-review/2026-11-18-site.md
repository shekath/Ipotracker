# UI review: stocks.shekathali.com (classic MarketLens)

Ready to ship: yes, with fixes. No blocking findings. While loading, two screens show an empty result, and when the API fails, Weekly targets shows a month-old trading signal (should-fix 1–9, notes 10–14, 3 questions for a person).

## Review record

- **Source:** live code at `stocks-classic` (deployed bundle `index-DdbMp6hz.js`), built locally. `/api` GETs were answered by the live site, and every other host (Google Tag Manager, GA4) was blocked, so no analytics hits were sent and no writes were possible (the app has none).
- **Tool:** Playwright Chromium, axe-core 4.10.2, the Event Timing API, CDP CPU throttling. Scripts are in the scratchpad (`uir-tools/capture.cjs`, `uir-tools/probe.cjs`).
- **Widths and themes:** 390 (isMobile, hasTouch, light) and 1280 (light and dark). Reduced motion was on for the captures.
- **Date:** 2026-11-18. **Criteria:** `ui-review/references/criteria.md`, plus the project's own rule in `src/index.css`: on a coarse pointer, controls are at least 44 px.
- **Routes:** Dashboard `/`, Picks `/picks`, Stocks `/stocks`, Screener `/screener`, IPO `/ipo`, Weekly targets `/weekly-targets` (the nav's top routes).
- **Purpose (assumed):** read research on Indian and US stocks: today's picks with trade plans, every stock's score, a full-market screener, live IPOs, and this week's index levels.
- **Lint:** the project has no lint script; its check is `npx tsc -b`, exit 0.
- **Gaps:** no panel (single reviewer). The stock detail page was not reviewed in depth (title only). US market views were not captured. Offline was not probed.

## Findings

### should-fix

1. **While loading, IPO and Stocks say there is nothing.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criterion 7.* With `/api` delayed by 8 s, the IPO desk shows "Nothing here · No IPOs match this status" under "Connecting to live feed…" (`state-slow-ipo-390.png`). Stocks shows "0 results" (`state-slow-stocks-390.png`). Picks shows an empty white card (`state-slow-picks-390.png`). A visitor on a slow connection is told there are no IPOs on the one page that exists to list them. *Fix:* the IPO list and the stocks count show a loading state (not "Nothing here" or "0 results") until the first response arrives.

2. **When the API fails, a stale signal reads as current.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criteria 7 and 8.* With `/api` returning 500, Weekly targets falls back to the 19–23 Oct snapshot: "Nifty 50 · current close 22,749.75" and a red "Below the pivot · Watch the downside targets" panel. Nifty actually closed at 25,387, which is above its pivot. The only sign is a small "Snapshot" chip and a 12 px line (`state-error-weekly-targets-390.png`). Stocks similarly shows June prices, flagged only in the subtitle (`state-error-stocks-390.png`). On a trading page, a month-old "downside" call is the opposite of today's. It is should-fix, not blocking, because the site is educational and no order is placed. *Fix:* a snapshot older than one week shows a banner at the top of the card naming its date, and drops the directional "Watch the … targets" call.

3. **The IPO failure looks like an empty market.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criterion 7.* The error state shows "Live feed offline" and then "Nothing here · No IPOs match this status" (`state-error-ipo-390.png`). The user can't tell "no IPOs" from "couldn't load". *Fix:* when the feed is offline and nothing is cached, the empty state says the feed couldn't be reached and offers a retry.

4. **Every route has the same page title.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criteria 1 and 6.* `document.title` is "MarketLens · Stocks & IPO Research" on `/`, `/picks`, `/ipo` and `/stocks/IN/TCS` (`probes.json` → titles). Browser tabs, history and screen-reader announcements can't tell pages apart. GA4 `page_title` is also identical for every page, so the Pages report can't use titles. *Fix:* each route sets a title that names it, such as "Picks · MarketLens" or "TCS · MarketLens".

5. **Typing in the screener search lags on a slower phone.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criterion 13, trap/press-delayed.* At 4× CPU throttle, typing "tata" in the Search company field on `/screener` (All India, 5,093 rows) gave key events up to **192 ms** over 17 events (`probes.json` → throttledScreenerTyping). Unthrottled presses elsewhere measured 24–32 ms. *Fix:* filter on a deferred value or a debounce, so every key event stays under 100 ms at 4× throttle.

6. **Search and select fields zoom the page on iPhone.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criterion 12; edge case "no input zoom on focus".* At 390 with touch, the Search company field and the Screen, Industry and Rows-per-page selects on `/screener` have a 14 px font (`probes.json` → touch.inputs). iOS Safari zooms any field under 16 px on focus, which shifts the layout. *Fix:* on a coarse pointer, `.input` and selects compute to 16 px or more.

7. **Table controls are below the project's 44 px touch rule.** Raised by: reviewer. *Criterion 12; project rule (`index.css`, coarse pointer ≥ 44 px).* At 390 with a coarse pointer, these are too small:
   - The sort headers ("Company", "Price", "Day", "P/E"…) are 16 px tall on `/stocks` and `/screener`.
   - The symbol links in rows are 17–21 px tall on `/`, `/picks`, `/stocks` and `/screener` (`audit.json` → smallSample: 17, 10, 27 and 44 controls per page).
   
   Row spacing keeps them clear of WCAG's 24 px minimum, so this applies the project's own stricter rule. *Fix:* on a coarse pointer, the sort header buttons and row links compute to a 44 px hit area (padding or the whole row as the target). This is `system-caused`, in the shared table styles.

8. **Some text fails contrast in light mode.** Raised by: reviewer, axe-core. *Criterion: contrast (ranked). `system-caused`:* two token pairs.
   - **Warning chips**: `warn` #B45309 on its 10% tint, 4.14–4.39:1. Seen on restriction flags such as "Trade-to-trade" and "Snapshot" on `/screener`, `/stocks` and `/weekly-targets`.
   - **Secondary text** (`sub` #647083) on tinted cards, 4.37–4.42:1: the Picks plan rows and the Weekly targets pivot panel. Also `up` text on `up/20` at 4.49:1 ("In play").
   
   Evidence: `audit.json`, 1280 and 390, light. *Fix:* each of these pairs measures 4.5:1 or more with axe `color-contrast`, for example by darkening the chip text or using solid chip backgrounds.

9. **The Weekly targets "passed" cards fail contrast in dark mode.** Raised by: reviewer, axe-core. *Criterion: contrast.* At 1280 dark, the dimmed cards (`opacity-60`) and the `ring-line` chips render #5B616E on #0E1017–#13161E, 2.91–3.05:1 (10 nodes, `targets-1280-dark.png`). The levels already passed become hard to read. *Fix:* dim with a lighter text token instead of opacity, so these nodes measure 4.5:1 or more.

### note

10. **The screener's Top gainer and Top loser values are cut off.** *Criterion 11.* At 1280, the All India summary shows "AMRAAGRI +2…" and "GEETANJ -23…" (`screener-1280-light.png`). The accessibility tree has the full "+25.0%" and "-23.8%". At 390 the values show in full. The number is the point of the stat. It's a note because the symbol still shows. *Fix:* the percentage never truncates at 1280; truncate the symbol first, or wrap.

11. **The screener's Industry column is empty for All India.** *Criterion 11.* Every visible row shows "—" in Industry at 1280 (`screener-1280-light.png`, 25 dashes in the tree). A full-width column with no data looks broken. *Fix:* hide the column when it has no values in the current list, or fill it from the research data.

12. **The dashboard's stock counts disagree.** *Criterion 10.* The "Stocks researched" card reads **12**, "NSE large caps with full analysis", while the section right below says "Top-ranked of **752** stocks" (`dashboard-1280-light.png`). The hero copy still says "index screeners from Nifty 50 to Nifty 200", but the screener now covers 5,093 stocks. Which number to show is a product call (see question 1).

13. **There's no skip link.** *Keyboard (ranked).* The first Tab lands on the logo. Reaching page content on `/picks` takes 10 stops (logo, 6 nav links, 3 theme buttons) on every route (`probes.json` → keyboardPicks). The focus rings are visible: 2–3 px solid on every stop (`probe-keyboard-picks-tab3-1280.png`). It's a note because the header is short and landmarks exist. *Fix:* the first Tab reaches "Skip to content".

14. **Heading levels skip, and one table can't be scrolled by keyboard.** *Semantics only (ranked).*
    - Card titles are `h3` directly under the page `h1` on `/picks`, `/stocks` and `/ipo` (axe `heading-order`).
    - At 390, the IPO "Big-ticket pipeline" table scrolls sideways but can't take keyboard focus (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`).
    
    *Fix:* the card titles become `h2`, and the scrolling wrapper gets `tabIndex=0` with a label.

## Edge cases

- **Shown:**
  - **Loading and slow loading:** findings 1 and 2.
  - **Error:** findings 2 and 3.
  - **Widths:** narrowest and widest. No horizontal page overflow at 390 or 1280 on any route (`audit.json`: overflow 0).
  - **Theme:** dark mode.
  - **Reduced motion:** on for every capture.
  - **Throttled CPU:** finding 5.
  - **Touch:** input zoom (finding 6). Tab bar targets are 78×56 and the header buttons 44×44, which passes.
  - **Long names:** wrap on Picks ("SRI LOTUS DEVELOPERS AND REALTY LTD.").
  - **An empty state** that says what goes here: Watchlist and US Swing (0).
- **Not shown (questions for the designer):**
  - Offline.
  - Partial data, other than the stale snapshot.
  - A returning user with a large watchlist.
  - The 768–1024 layout switch.
  - Repeated quick presses.
  - Hover sticking on touch.
  - The stock detail page, and the US market views.

## For a person to decide

1. Which "stocks researched" number the dashboard should show: 12 (analyst-covered), 752 (scored), or 5,093 (screened), and whether the hero copy should mention the full market.
2. Whether a stale weekly-targets snapshot should still show a directional call at all, or only the levels (finding 2).
3. Whether to keep GA4 `page_title` as-is or set per-route titles (finding 4). The fix changes what the Analytics Pages report groups by.

## Dismissed and left out

- **Logo "Lens" at 2.38:1** (axe, every page): dismissed. Text in a logo or brand name is exempt from WCAG 1.4.3.
- **The fixed tab bar appearing mid-page in the full-page 390 captures:** dismissed. It's a screenshot artifact of a fixed element, not seen in the viewport captures.
- **Nav names like "Weekly targetsWeekly index targets"** from `textContent`: dismissed. The accessibility tree names it correctly ("Today's picks 12").
- **Console errors:** none observed. Not traced further.
